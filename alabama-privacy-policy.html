Privacy Policy

Alabama Building Materials Trading L.L.C. · Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Alabama Building Materials Trading L.L.C. may collect, use, store and share information when you visit our website, contact our team, or send a product or project enquiry.

Alabama Building Materials Trading L.L.C. (“Alabama”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a building materials supplier based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. We supply plumbing and building materials, including water heaters, pipes and fittings, valves, pumps and sanitaryware, for residential, commercial and industrial projects.

We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly and transparently. This policy should be read alongside any specific notice shown when you use a particular form or service.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, business name, email address, telephone number and preferred contact method.
  • Enquiry and project information: product interests, specifications, project requirements, delivery details and the contents of messages you send us.
  • Website and device information: pages visited, referring pages, browser and device information, approximate location derived from technical data, and timestamps or diagnostic logs.
  • Cookie and measurement data: information collected through cookies or similar technologies where used and permitted by your browser settings and applicable law.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through general enquiry forms unless it is genuinely necessary and we have requested it.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide quotations, product details or technical assistance;
  • manage orders, deliveries, customer relationships and after-sales support;
  • communicate about a request, order or business relationship;
  • operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve our website and services;
  • understand website usage and measure marketing performance, subject to applicable consent requirements;
  • meet legal obligations, protect our rights and prevent misuse or fraud.

We do not state that every activity applies to every visitor; the information used depends on the service or interaction involved.

3. Legal basis and applicable law

Where applicable, we process personal information on a lawful basis such as responding to your request, performing or preparing to perform a contract, complying with legal obligations, pursuing legitimate business interests, or obtaining consent where required. We aim to handle personal information in accordance with applicable UAE data-protection requirements, including the UAE Personal Data Protection Law where it applies.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising measurement

Our website may use essential technologies to support site functionality and, where configured, analytics or marketing measurement tools such as Google Tag Manager and tags deployed through it. Google Tag Manager is a tag-management tool; the information collected depends on which tags are configured and how they operate.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Where applicable law requires consent before non-essential cookies or tracking technologies are used, those technologies should only be activated in accordance with the relevant consent requirements. Blocking certain technologies may affect some website features.

5. When we may share information

We may share relevant information with service providers who help us operate our website, communications, analytics, hosting, customer support, logistics or business systems; with manufacturers or project partners where needed to respond to a request; and with regulators, authorities or other parties where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights. We expect service providers to handle information appropriately and only for authorised purposes.

We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

6. International transfers

Some service providers may process information in countries outside the United Arab Emirates. Where this occurs, we aim to use appropriate safeguards and comply with applicable legal requirements for cross-border transfers.

7. Data retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, including responding to enquiries, maintaining business records, meeting legal requirements and resolving disputes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the circumstances.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also be able to raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and some information may need to be retained where the law permits or requires it.

9. Third-party websites

Our website may link to third-party websites or services. Those services have their own privacy practices, and we are not responsible for their content or policies. Please review their privacy notices before sharing information with them.

10. Children’s privacy

Our website and business services are intended for business customers and the general public, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review the matter.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology or legal obligations. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
